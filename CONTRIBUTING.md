# Contributing to Open Tomato

Everyone is welcome to open issues and comment. Bug reports, questions and
ideas from outside the team are read by people, and they matter to us.

## Issues, comments and our agents

Open Tomato builds tools for AI agents, and our own agents work from these
boards. That makes issue threads a target: automated accounts watch for new
issues on certain topics and reply within minutes with promotions, sign-up
links or text written to steer an agent. To keep the boards useful and our
agents safe, these rules apply to every issue, comment and pull request
description in every Open Tomato repository.

### What our agents read

Our agents read only issues and comments written by Open Tomato maintainers
(accounts with write access to the repository). Text from anyone else is
filtered out before an agent sees it. Your contribution still reaches a
person, because a maintainer reads it and carries it over when it shapes the
work.

### What we redact or hide

Maintainers, and the automation acting for them, may do the following
without notice:

1. **Links.** A link to a host outside our allowed list is redacted and
   replaced with `[link removed by maintainers: host not on the allowed
   list]`. The list holds GitHub, Open Tomato's own sites and packages, and
   the documentation of widely used frameworks and tools. If you need to
   share something else, say so in the thread, and a maintainer can add the
   host.
2. **Promotion.** Offers of services, tools or integrations, and requests to
   recruit testers or users, are hidden as spam.
3. **Text aimed at agents.** Instructions addressed to AI agents, assistants
   or bots, and wording that tries to change or override what they were told
   to do, are removed.
4. **Hidden content.** A comment must read the same rendered and raw. HTML
   comments, invisible or zero-width characters, Unicode tag characters,
   right-to-left override controls, and markup that hides text from readers
   are removed.

Redactions are marked in the comment itself. A comment hidden as spam can
still be expanded by anyone who can read the repository. An account that
keeps posting such content is blocked from the organization.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's **Report content** on any comment that breaks these rules. For
a security problem, open a private security advisory on the repository; do
not describe it in a public issue.
